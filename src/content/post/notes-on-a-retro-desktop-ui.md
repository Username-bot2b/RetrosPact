Modern websites often compete for attention with motion, gradients, and oversized type. A retro desktop look does the opposite: it frames content like applications on a desk.

A few rules that helped this theme:

1. **One window, one job** — each section is a framed surface, not a card collage.
2. **No soft animation** — clicks and menus respond immediately.
3. **Warm system colors** — beige faces, brown title bars, and high-contrast bevels.

It is not nostalgia for its own sake. It is a constraint that keeps the page quiet enough to read.
