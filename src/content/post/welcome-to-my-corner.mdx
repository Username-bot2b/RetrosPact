This site is a small personal space — notes, experiments, and whatever I am learning.

The UI borrows from classic desktop aesthetics (in the spirit of SerenityOS): windows, title bars, and instant controls instead of glossy marketing pages.

If you are reading this, you found the sample blog. The sections below are a **live style check** for HTML tags and MDX inside the retro theme.
