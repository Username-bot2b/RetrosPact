Astro works well for personal sites because most pages can be static HTML. You still get Markdown/MDX for writing, image optimization, and room for small interactive islands when needed (for example, comments).

A practical setup:

- **Home** — short intro and recent posts
- **About** — who you are
- **Blog** — long-form notes
- **Contact** — a simple form or email link

Keep the information architecture small. A personal site rarely needs landing pages, pricing tables, or template showrooms.
